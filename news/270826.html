Vs. Al3x

I was not expecting the release of this mod to be so great, but almost three years after the mod page had been created, we have finally released Vs. Al3x.

Gameplay Video

The best part is that this mod only took a couple months to complete, two of the songs (Bleepbloop and Bugfix) were already complete because they were scrapped from Vs. Al3x Robot Repolished which is what this turned into.

Al3x's art took the longest to complete, it was started on June 14th when I traced the new design that freble made for Al3x months prior for 'Robot Repolished'.

Bugfix Pico Mix was finished on the 17th of June and Bleepbloop Erect was completed on the 17th of July, then the mod was released on the 17th of August.

Weird huh?

We're hoping to release Vs. Al3x v1.1.0 for October 2026, but no promises! :D
