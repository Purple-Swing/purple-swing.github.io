Great Flarp Birb

Our older promises that Great Flarp Birb (GFB) would be recreated in HaxeFlixel have been repealed. We can now claim that GFB v2.0.0 will be developed in Unity using Reign.

The development of GFB is going very well, there is now enough progress on the project that we can reveal what is so different with this new update.

Our Goals

We have a large amount of goals to fulfill with GFB, here is a small portion of said list:

  • Gamemodes, in which each one functions entirely different to one another
  • A soundtrack menu for people to listen to the beautifully composed music
  • Humorous antics involving Birb (analogue smiley face emoji)

Flarp Birb Horror

We are proud to announce that Flarp Birb Horror is receiving a refining update.

This update will polish Flarp Birb Horror from being an ironic indie horror experience to a standard short semi-satire escape horror game.

We cannot show much as of now, but we can show the menu in its current state.

Flarp Birb Horror 2

There is not much information about Flarp Birb Horror 2 yet, the story and overarching gameplay mechanics are still being discussed and finalised before development begins.

Expect more realism and less "fun" than Flarp Birb Horror 1.

Conclusion

We hope this scheduled update has revealed enough information and hope to see you playing Vs Al3x!

Postscript: we will be taking a break for a month or so to fully recuperate our energy.
