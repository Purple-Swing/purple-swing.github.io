The Current Scope

So... you may ask, 'What have you been doing? Where are my games?' We're workin' on it!


Currently, Flarp Birb Horror v0.7.0 is around 40% done, whereas Great Flarp Birb 2 v2.0 is around 20-30% done.

Except: we're not rushing this time, we want to make sure that these games get the treatment they deserve.

We also plan to release Mac and Linux ports of all of our games, new and old! But we can not disclose as to when any of the previously listed projects will be complete.

Internally, there has also been discussion on how to price said games. To which we have decided Great Flarp Birb 2 will be free and that Flarp Birb Horror 2's price will increase with the content we add after v0.7.0, from £6.00 to a maximum of £10.00 when fully complete.

POSTBOX Returns...

Yep, you heard that right, it's that time of year yet again! As well as all of the current work on mandatory projects, we will be spending some time in the music department, back where the trio began.

Our new EP, Point of Reflection, is out now. A wave goodbye to the old era of POSTBOX.

As we continue to grow frail and old, we'll work on this as a side-project, we still care very much about it - it technically brought us all together like bees in a hive community and all that nonsense.

We still have lots of things in our discography that have been in a 'development hell' kind of state for a while, after-thoughts, so prepare!
